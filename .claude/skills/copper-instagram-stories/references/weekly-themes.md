# Weekly themes

Seven fixed slots, rotating content. Each sequence is 4–6 frames with one main action. Frame skeletons are defaults; change them when the material calls for it, but keep "motion hook → value + one sticker → one call to action".

Copper books Wednesday–Saturday, so Monday and Tuesday use footage and notes from the previous week. Hard selling is limited to Friday and Sunday; other days close softly.

Highlight codes: **Book**, **Services**, **Results**, **Reviews**, **FAQ**, **About**.

---

## Monday · Shelf Talk (product education)

**Goal:** Mia learns one thing that protects her hair at home and trusts Copper's judgment. Also the day Copper gets the weekly intake message at noon (see `monday-intake.md`).

**Topic bank:** why dark hair goes brassy by week 3 and what a toner/purple shampoo does; bond builder in every lightening service; vegan color; extension-safe brush and detangling (most K-tip damage comes from detangling habits); keeping oils and heavy conditioners off bonds and tape; not sleeping with wet extensions; heat protectant; silk pillowcase; the take-home duo Copper actually recommends.

**Skeleton:**
1. Hook clip: product in hand or the problem on real hair. "Why your blonde turns brassy by week 3."
2. The cause, one line, on cream.
3. The fix: product with label visible, brand tagged. Disclosure if gifted/affiliate.
4. How to use it, one step, short demo clip. Quiz or poll sticker ("How often do you tone at home?").
5. Close: "Reply 'SHELF' and I'll tell you what your hair needs" or link to book.

**Sticker:** quiz or poll. **Highlight:** FAQ or Services.

---

## Tuesday · Off the Clock (personal)

**Goal:** Mia feels she knows Copper, the person she'd trust for years. The one fully personal day.

**What fits:** what Copper's been up to or into: a class or certification, a brand education day, a new technique being practiced, a hike, a matcha or brunch spot at Spectrum, a trip, a book or podcast, a favorite local shop, a morning routine, a pet. Choose things Mia also likes (wellness, food, travel, style, Orange County). Tie back to hair lightly in the last frame if natural ("back in the chair Wednesday").

**Avoid:** politics, relationship drama, client gossip, health crises, complaints about no-shows or other salons, anything Copper hasn't okayed sharing.

**Skeleton:**
1. Motion clip of the moment (walking into the place, pouring the matcha, the trail view). One short line.
2. Why it matters to Copper, one sentence, Copper's face if possible.
3. Emoji slider or Add Yours ("Your Spectrum lunch spot?").
4. Optional: a second moment.
5. Soft close: "Back behind the chair Wednesday ✦ DM me anytime."

**Sticker:** emoji slider, poll or Add Yours. **Highlight:** About (only the best ones).

---

## Wednesday · Ask Copper (FAQ / myth-busting)

**Goal:** answer one real question honestly and move unsure viewers to a consultation.

**Topic bank:** "Will extensions damage my hair?"; sewn nano weft vs K-tip vs tape (in priority order, honest about who suits what); "Can dark hair go blonde without turning orange?"; "How long does keratin last?"; "Am I a candidate for extensions if my hair is thin?"; "What happens at a consultation?"; price transparency (only confirmed "starting at" figures).

Use questions from last week's question box when available; otherwise use the bank.

**Skeleton:**
1. The question as a big serif headline over a hair clip.
2. Copper's answer on camera (captions sticker on), or 2 text frames.
3. The myth vs the truth, side by side.
4. Quiz sticker or question box ("What should I answer next week?").
5. Close: "Not sure you're a candidate? Start with a consultation, link here" (New Client page) or the free Hair & Scalp analysis.

**Sticker:** question box or quiz. **Highlight:** FAQ.

---

## Thursday · 15-Minute Hair (styling tips)

**Goal:** a style Mia can actually do in 15 minutes, which also shows off Copper's work.

**Topic bank:** heatless waves overnight; gym-to-dinner ponytail that hides extension wefts; sleek low bun; 10-minute blowout with a round brush; reviving day-3 hair; styling around a toddler (fast, low-effort); wedding-guest half-up; caring for extensions after Pilates or a hike (rinse sweat, dry roots fully).

**Skeleton:**
1. The finished style, motion, with the hook: "15-min heatless waves, gym to dinner."
2–4. One step per frame, short clip + 6–10 words each. Tools shown.
5. Extension or color caution if relevant ("keep the iron off the bonds").
6. Poll ("Want the 2nd-day version?") or question box; point to the Styling/FAQ Highlight.

**Sticker:** poll or question box. **Highlight:** FAQ (styling) or Services.

---

## Friday · Kind Words & Reveals (proof + weekend openings)

**Goal:** proof that converts, plus a reason to book this weekend.

**What fits:** matched before/after of a client who looks like Mia (dark hair, extensions or balayage), a branded review card (real review, first name + last initial), the service named exactly as listed, weekend openings.

**Skeleton:**
1. Reveal clip: hair flip or 360 turn, no filter.
2. The before (same angle and light).
3. What was done, one line, service as listed ("Custom extensions · sewn nano weft").
4. Review card on mid/deep copper.
5. Openings: "Two spots open Saturday" + countdown or link sticker to the exact service. If fully booked: "Booked this weekend. Reply 'NEXT' for next week."

**Sticker:** link (+ countdown when there's a deadline). **Highlight:** Results, Reviews, Book.

---

## Saturday · In the Chair (behind the chair)

**Goal:** show precision and patience in action, live from House of Lange; fill same-day gaps.

**What fits:** consult clip (Copper explaining what's realistic), sectioning, nano-weft sewing, bond builder going in, toning, keratin application, the blow-dry, the salon and Spectrum setting. Client consent before showing faces.

**Skeleton:**
1. Mid-service motion: "Morning at House of Lange ✦ sewing in today's length."
2. Process step, one line of why.
3. Emoji slider ("Rate this melt") or poll.
4. Optional: a second step or the finish preview (save the full reveal for Friday).
5. Close: "Had a cancellation at 3:30, DM 'TODAY'" only if true; otherwise "Booking Wednesdays–Saturdays, link here."

**Sticker:** emoji slider or poll. **Highlight:** Services.

---

## Sunday · Sunday Reset (trends + next week's openings)

**Goal:** a light trend conversation, then next week's availability.

**Topic bank:** seasonal trends that suit Mia (lived-in blonde vs glossy brunette, soft copper for fall, "expensive brunette", face-framing, curtain bangs, pre-holiday color timing, pre-vacation keratin, "New Year, new length"); a mood board from Pinterest-style inspo (credited); one tip for the week ahead.

**Skeleton:**
1. Trend moodboard clip or photo, credited if not Copper's work.
2. Poll ("Lived-in blonde or glossy brunette?").
3. Copper's honest take: who it suits, what it takes.
4. Next week's openings as a clean list (Wed–Sat).
5. Link sticker to booking or "Reply 'WEEK' for times."

**Sticker:** poll, then link. **Highlight:** Book (openings frame only if evergreen; usually skip).

## Seasonal overlays

Layer onto the rotation without breaking it:

- **Nov–Dec:** holiday photos, events, pre-holiday booking ("book by…" only with real dates).
- **Jan:** "New Year, new length" campaign (from the 90-day plan).
- **Spring:** wedding season prep (bride-to-be), pre-summer keratin.
- **Summer:** travel hair, sweat-proof extension care, sun and chlorine protection.
- **Aug–Sep:** back-to-routine, fall color.

## If the week is short on material

Drop Wednesday, then Saturday. Keep Friday (proof) and Monday (education). A text-only frame on espresso or cream with a big serif headline and Copper's voice is always acceptable as a fallback, as long as frame 1 still has some motion (a short hair clip behind a text block works).
