## Her hair

She's worth about $5,900–$6,140 a year: sewn nano-weft extensions (move-ups every 8 weeks), balayage about 4× a year, keratin before summer, treatment add-ons, take-home products.

## What drives her

She wants:
- Hair that looks expensive but natural; extensions nobody can spot.
- Healthy hair after lightening (bond builder, vegan color).
- A style she can recreate in 15 minutes.
- A stylist who listens and is honest about what's realistic.
- One trusted stylist for years.

She's been burned by:
- Brassy or orange results when dark hair was lifted.
- Heavy, visible or damaging extensions from a cheaper salon.
- Feeling rushed, ignored or upsold.
- Surprise prices at checkout.

What gets her to book:
1. A before/after on hair that looks like hers.
2. Reviews that name Copper and mention patience and results.
3. A clear first step: the New Client page or a consultation.
4. A Saturday or weekday-afternoon slot, easy Spectrum parking, dinner nearby.

Messages that land: "Length that looks like yours." "Blonde on dark hair, done gently." "I'll tell you exactly what your hair can do."
