# Story craft: specs and evidence

Based on the research report "Instagram Stories for hairstylists" (October 2026). Most numbers come from vendor blogs, not Instagram, and none were measured on salons. Treat them as defaults to test; Copper's own Insights override them after about a month.

## How Stories are ranked

- Stories mostly reach people who already follow you; they're "less suited to discovery" (Mosseri, 2025). Top signals: how likely a viewer is to tap into the Story, like it, and reply.
- Since September 12, 2025, posting several Stories a day no longer cuts reach (Instagram fixed a bug). Daily multi-frame sequences are fine.
- Small accounts (<5K followers) reach roughly 2–10% of followers per Story. Plan for small audiences and make each one count: replies and DMs both feed ranking and close bookings.

## Sequence length

- **4–6 frames a day, 7 maximum.** Retention stays above ~75% through 7 frames; the biggest drop is between frame 1 and 2 (~24% exit on frame 1).
- **Frame 1 moves.** Video holds better than stills; put motion in the first 3 seconds (hair flip, reveal, swing).
- **Order:** hook → 1–3 value frames with one sticker (frame 2–3) → call to action (frame 3–5). Link sticker not on frame 1.
- Photo frames display ~5–7 seconds; videos up to 60 seconds per frame.

## Canvas and safe zones

| Spec | Rule |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1080x1920 (9:16) |
| Text + sticker band | y = 250 to 1580 |
| Side margins | ≥60px (brand layout uses 96px) |
| Stickers | Middle third, ~y = 700–1300 |
| Words per photo frame | 6–12; up to ~25 only on a dedicated text card |
| Body type | ≥48px (56–64 preferred) |
| Headlines | 90–140px |
| Smallest text | Labels/credits only, ≥30px |
| Fonts | 2 max |
| Contrast | ≥4.5:1 normal text, ≥3:1 display text (72px+) |

Instagram publishes no official organic safe zone; these are conservative community values. Check a frame on a real phone when in doubt.

## Stickers

- **Poll, emoji slider:** quick taps; good for frames 2–3 and light topics.
- **Question box:** fewer but richer replies; turns into DMs and next Wednesday's FAQ.
- **Quiz:** myth-busting.
- **Countdown:** real deadlines and openings only.
- **Link:** expect ~0.3–1.2% of viewers to tap; link straight to the exact service or the New Client page, not a link hub.
- **DM keyword** ("Reply 'LENGTH'"): produces replies, which help ranking and let Copper qualify the client. Alternate with link CTAs and compare.
- **Add Yours:** post-appointment selfies, Tuesday prompts.
- **Mention:** tag the client (with consent), @houseoflange, product brands.
- **Captions sticker:** on every talking clip; proofread.
- One interactive sticker per frame; don't stack.

## Posting times (Pacific), until Insights say otherwise

Split each sequence across the day when possible:

- **7:30–9 a.m.:** hook + today's openings (2–3 frames)
- **12–1 p.m.:** one frame (mid-service clip or poll); this is Mia's lunch break
- **5–7 p.m.:** reveal + question box

After ~4 weeks, switch to the account's "Most active times" (or Metricool's best-time data if connected).

## Highlights

Booking-first order: **Book** (how to book, policies, New Client page), **Services** (prices only if confirmed), **Results**, **Reviews**, **FAQ**, **About**. One consistent cover style: espresso circle, cream serif or simple line icon. Check Book and Services monthly; outdated promos hurt trust.

## Shooting hair

- Indirect natural daylight, client facing the light. Side light shows dimension; overhead light flattens; backlight exaggerates frizz. Salon bulbs shift tone.
- Plain background, cape on for befores, ring light only for evening clients.
- Before/after: same angle, distance, light. No beauty filters; saying "no filter" is worth it.
- Extensions: waves/curls blend best; check wefts before movement shots.
- Balayage: show movement and the root melt; a slow turn or flip in daylight.
- Dark-base lightening, glass-hair keratin and extensions on Asian hair textures are strong for Irvine (about 45% Asian American); low-damage, scalp-health messaging fits K-beauty-minded clients.

## Compliance

- **Disclosure:** "Gifted by [Brand]" or "Ad" on the frame itself, high contrast, readable; Instagram's paid-partnership label alone may not be enough.
- **Reviews:** FTC rule in force since Oct 21, 2024 bans fake or AI-written reviews, sentiment-conditioned incentives and bought followers/views.
- **Client photos:** signed release covering Instagram, before/afters, tagged vs anonymous, and how to withdraw. California publicity/privacy specifics weren't researched; ask counsel if unsure.
- Don't use the unsourced "82% of salon clients check Instagram before booking" stat.

## Metrics to log per sequence

1. Reach as % of followers
2. Frame-1 exits
3. Completion to last frame
4. Sticker taps + replies
5. Link clicks on the CTA frame

Re-rank themes, sticker types, CTA types (link vs DM keyword) and posting windows monthly.
