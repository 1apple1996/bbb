# Monday intake

Every Monday at noon (Pacific) Copper gets a check-in asking for this week's material. The goal is to make it a two-minute reply, not homework.

## The check-in message

**Ask one question at a time** (Copper's preference): send question 1, wait for the reply, file it, then send the next. Keep each one short and specific to the coming week (season, last week's gaps, upcoming holidays). The full list, in order:

> Hi Copper, it's Stories planning time for the week of **[Mon date]**. Send whatever's easy, photos, clips, voice notes or a few words:
>
> 1. **Results:** any before/afters from last week you're proud of (client okayed for Instagram?)
> 2. **Kind words:** a new review or client text you can share (first name + last initial)
> 3. **Product:** one product you've been recommending, and is it gifted or affiliate?
> 4. **Questions clients asked** in the chair this week
> 5. **You:** anything you did or got into off the clock (class, trip, spot, book, hobby)
> 6. **Openings:** any gaps Wed–Sat this week, and anything already full?
> 7. **Inspo:** any trend, look or account you've been loving
>
> (With the first question, mention once: "Short on time? Say 'skip' to any question, or just send openings and one photo.")

If last week's results are known, add one line on what worked ("Thursday's heatless-waves poll got the most replies").

## Filing what Copper sends

Map each item to a day:

| Material | Day |
|---|---|
| Product, care tip | Mon · Shelf Talk |
| Personal moment, class, trip, spot | Tue · Off the Clock |
| Client questions | Wed · Ask Copper |
| Styling clip, how-to | Thu · 15-Minute Hair |
| Before/after, review | Fri · Kind Words & Reveals |
| Process clips, consults | Sat · In the Chair |
| Trend, inspo, openings for next week | Sun · Sunday Reset |

Note for each item: client consent (yes/unknown), gifted/affiliate (yes/no), and any facts to confirm (price, NAHA wording, dates).

## Then

Once Copper replies, plan the week (SKILL.md steps 3–7). If Copper sends nothing by the time a day's Story is needed, use the topic bank in `weekly-themes.md` with clearly marked placeholders and a shot list, and say which frames still need real photos.
