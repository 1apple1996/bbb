## Voice

- First person as Copper. Warm, precise, calm, honest. Short sentences.
- Explain the why in one line ("bond builder protects the hair while it lifts").
- Honest about limits ("if your hair is very fine, I'll start with fewer rows").
- No hype words ("obsessed!!!", "life-changing"), no pressure ("last chance!"), no emoji-heavy text. One tasteful symbol (✦) is fine.
- Calls to action are invitations: "Reply 'LENGTH' and I'll send you times." "Start on the New Client page, link here."

## Availability language

Talk about availability like a sought-after specialist, never like someone with an empty book (Copper's rule). Present it as a service detail, clearly and calmly; don't fake scarcity.

- **Use:** "Now booking Nov 11–14", "Available today · 2–7 p.m.", "Select times available Thursday", "Reserve your Friday", "Holiday appointments now booking", "Booking Wednesdays–Saturdays", "Reply 'BLONDE' and I'll send you times", "Next week's appointments post tomorrow".
- **Never use:** "wide open", "completely open", "has room", "plenty of openings", "slow week", "lots of availability", "last-minute openings", "please book", "need clients", or anything that sounds like the book is empty.
- Say "limited", "only a few left" or "almost booked" only when it's true that day.
- "Fully booked today" is good when true; follow it with when Copper is next available.

## Type

- **Headlines:** Cormorant Garamond 500 (italic 500 for emphasis), 90–140px, line-height ~0.95–1.05.
- **Body:** Montserrat 400/500, 48px minimum (56–64px preferred), line-height ~1.4.
- **Eyebrow labels:** Montserrat 600, 30px, uppercase, letter-spacing 8px, accent color. (Labels are the only text allowed under 48px, never under 30px.)
- Two fonts only. No script fonts.
- Google Fonts link: `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,500;0,600;1,500&family=Montserrat:wght@400;500;600&display=swap`

## Layout signatures

- Padding: 260px top, 96px sides, 340px bottom (inside the safe band).
- Eyebrow label at top, then headline, then content.
- Thin 2px accent line (120px wide) before sign-offs like "Irvine Spectrum · House of Lange".
- Rounded 28px corners on photo panels.
- Numbered lists use small accent numerals ("01", "02") above serif subheads, divided by 2px hairlines.
- Stars and icons as simple inline SVG, never emoji.

## Proof rules

- Reviews: real, verbatim, first name + last initial (e.g. "Vicki S."), with the client's permission and ideally the source (Google, Yelp).
- Before/afters: same angle, distance and light; no filters; signed photo release on file.
- Gifted or affiliate products: "Gifted by [Brand]" or "Ad" on the frame, high contrast. Copper will say when a product is gifted or affiliate; don't ask.
