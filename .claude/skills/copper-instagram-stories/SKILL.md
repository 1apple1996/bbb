# Copper Aguirre Instagram Stories

This skill turns Copper's real material (client photos, clips, products, news) into a week of Stories that speak to Mia, look like the Copper Aguirre brand, and move warm followers toward a booking or a DM.

Stories are a conversion tool for people who already follow @copperaguirre, not a discovery channel: Instagram ranks Stories mainly on how likely a follower is to tap in, like, or reply. So every sequence is built to earn one reply, sticker tap or DM, and the week stays near 80% helpful/inspiring and 20% selling.

## Files in this skill

Read these as needed rather than all at once:

- `references/dream-client.md`: who Mia is, what she wants, what she has been burned by, the messages that land. Read before writing any copy.
- `references/brand.md`: palette (with contrast-checked pairs), type, voice, services as listed, extension priority, proof rules. Read before designing or writing.
- `references/weekly-themes.md`: the seven daily themes with frame-by-frame skeletons, stickers and calls to action. Read when planning a day or week.
- `references/story-craft.md`: sequence length, safe zones, type sizes, stickers, posting times, Highlights, shooting hair, compliance, metrics. Read before designing frames.
- `references/monday-intake.md`: the Monday check-in message and how to file what Copper sends. Read when it's Monday intake or Copper sends new material.
- `assets/frame-template.html`: a 1080x1920 frame template in the brand system, used when no design canvas tool is available.

## Workflow

### 1. Work out what's being asked

- **Monday intake** (a scheduled Monday check-in, or "what do you need from me this week?"): follow `references/monday-intake.md`. Ask, then stop; don't invent the week before Copper has replied, unless asked to plan with what's on hand.
- **A full week** ("plan this week's stories"): plan all seven days, then design them.
- **One day** ("what should I post today?", "make Thursday's story"): find today's theme in the table below and make just that sequence.
- **Material in hand** (Copper sends photos/clips/notes): map each item to the day it fits best, then fill gaps.

### 2. Gather what's real

Use only material Copper provided or facts already in the references. Every client photo, review, product and price must be real. When something is missing, put a clear placeholder in the frame, like `[BEFORE/AFTER: dark brown to soft balayage]` or `[CLIENT REVIEW, first name + last initial]`, and list it in the shot list. Never write a testimonial, invent a price, or make up a client result: fake or AI-written reviews are illegal under the FTC rule in force since October 2024, and Mia picks stylists on trust.

If the conversation has a week's intake (from the Monday check-in), use it. Before any frame shows hours, openings or booking times, check Copper's hours on copperaguirre.com (or ask Copper if the site can't be reached) and ask for current availability; never reuse old openings. If Metricool tools are connected, `getBestTimeToPostByNetwork` for Instagram can replace the default posting times.

### 3. Plan the week

Default rotation (Copper books Wednesday–Saturday, so Monday/Tuesday use footage from the previous week):

| Day | Theme | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Shelf Talk**: product education | Teach one product or care habit Mia can use at home |
| Tue | **Off the Clock**: personal | What Copper's been up to or into; builds trust |
| Wed | **Ask Copper**: FAQ / myth-busting | Answer a real question; push the consultation |
| Thu | **15-Minute Hair**: styling tips | A style Mia can do in 15 minutes |
| Fri | **Kind Words & Reveals**: proof | Before/after + review; weekend openings |
| Sat | **In the Chair**: behind the chair | Process, live from the salon; same-day openings |
| Sun | **Sunday Reset**: trends + next week | Trend poll; next week's openings |

Details per day are in `references/weekly-themes.md`. Busy week? Drop Wednesday or Saturday first; never drop Friday.

Before keeping any idea, ask: **"Would Mia save this, reply to it, or send it to a friend?"** If not, swap it.

### 4. Write each day's sequence

For every day, produce:

1. **Theme + one-line goal** (the single action wanted from viewers).
2. **Frames, 4–6** (7 maximum). For each frame: frame number, visual (the photo/clip or placeholder), on-screen text (exact words, 6–12 words on photo frames), sticker (if any, with exact wording/options), and where it goes after 24h (Highlight: Book, Services, Results, Reviews, FAQ, About, or none).
3. **Posting time** (Pacific) for the sequence or each part of it.
4. **Shot list** of anything Copper still needs to capture.
5. **Caption/alt notes**: captions sticker on any talking clip; FTC disclosure on any gifted/affiliate product frame.

The default shape: frame 1 is motion (a hair flip, reveal or swing) with a short hook; frames 2–3 deliver the value and carry one interactive sticker; frame 3–5 is the call to action (link sticker to the exact service or the New Client page, or "DM 'KEYWORD'"). Alternate link and DM-keyword calls to action across the week so Copper can see which works.

Voice: first person as Copper, warm, specific, honest about what hair can do, never pushy. Use Mia's language ("length that looks like yours", "blonde on dark hair, done gently") and avoid salon jargon unless it's explained in the same frame.

### 5. Design the frames

Every frame follows the brand system in `references/brand.md` and the specs in `references/story-craft.md`: 1080x1920, text and stickers only between y=250 and y=1580, at least 60px side margins, Cormorant Garamond headlines (90–140px) over Montserrat body (48px minimum), contrast-checked color pairs only, at most one sticker per frame. "Polished frame, real content": the template is refined and consistent, and the hair footage is real, unfiltered and well lit.

Where to build:

- **If the Artifact tool can create Design canvases**: make one canvas per week titled "Copper Stories · Week of <Mon date>", one 1080x1920 artboard per frame, a title note per day, and rows by day. If a "Copper Aguirre Instagram Stories" canvas exists, match its look (it's the brand reference) rather than editing it.
- **Otherwise**: copy `assets/frame-template.html` per frame (or one file with all frames stacked) and fill the slots.

**Photo edits need Copper's approval.** After cropping, blurring or otherwise editing any photo or video still, show Copper the edited versions and wait for approval before putting them in frames. Copper asked for this.

Leave sticker areas empty in the design (Copper adds real stickers in the Instagram app); mark them with a dashed outline labelled with the sticker's wording so placement is clear.

### 6. Quality check before handing over

Go through each frame:

- Would Mia stop on frame 1? Is there motion?
- One idea per frame; no paragraphs on photo frames.
- All text inside y=250–1580 and the side margins; type sizes at or above the minimums.
- Only approved color pairs (see brand.md); no body text on the mid-copper background.
- Real material or a clearly marked placeholder; no invented reviews, prices or results; NAHA wording matches what's confirmed.
- Services named exactly as listed; extension methods in priority order.
- Disclosure on gifted/affiliate frames; captions on talking clips; client consent noted for any client photo.
- Each day has exactly one main action, and the week has no more than two hard-sell days (Fri, Sun) plus soft closes.

### 7. Deliver

Give Copper: the week (or day) at a glance, the canvas link or files, the shot list of what's still needed, and the posting times. Keep the chat summary short; the frames are the deliverable. Don't post or schedule anything to Instagram or Metricool unless Copper explicitly asks for that specific week.

### 8. Learn from results

When Copper shares Insights, log per sequence: reach as % of followers, frame-1 exits, completion to the last frame, sticker taps + replies, and link clicks. Once a month, re-rank which themes, stickers, call-to-action types and times work, and let Copper's own numbers override the defaults in `references/story-craft.md`.
